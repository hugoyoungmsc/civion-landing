The spreadsheet tax

The checks are not the hard part. Rebuilding them is.

Every section starts the same way: copy the last spreadsheet, re-enter the geometry, remember which clause changed, and hope the hidden cells still hold.

01

Rebuilt from the last job

Geometry, materials and bar layouts are retyped for every section. One stale cell and the answer looks right for the wrong reason.

02

Assumptions buried in cells

Which national annex? Which strut angle? Which v_min? The spreadsheet remembers, but the report does not show it.

03

Reports assembled by hand

The calculation is done, then the report is written again — screenshots, tables and clause references pasted into Word.

Why not a free calculator?

How this differs from a free section calculator.

Free calculators are excellent when you need one check. Civion is built for the design you have to hand over.

  • It starts from load cases, not a single M or V you typed in.
  • It runs every load case and shows the utilisation of each.
  • It applies a named national-annex route — CEN or French NA; UK NA next — not just a set of NDP values.
  • It keeps the project, client and calculation history.
  • It exports a branded PDF and Excel workbook.

Use a free calculator when you need a number.
